Words that help

If it gets too much, you're allowed to say these. Practise them here.

“Can I use my time-out card, please?”
“Can I leave five minutes early today?”
“I'm finding this a lot right now. Can I have a minute?”
“I don't understand yet — can you show me again?”
“It's really loud. Can I wait till the corridor's quiet?”
